# No AI slop eval

Use this after an edit. Mark each check pass or fail; fix failures before returning the draft.

For Detect mode, verify that the report names each observed pattern, quotes the affected passage,
and gives a short fix without rewriting, scoring, or claiming authorship.

## Meaning and voice

1. Does the edit preserve the writer's point without adding claims, examples, statistics, quotes,
   sources, or opinions?
2. Does it preserve distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, digressions,
   and level of polish?
3. Were strong sentences left alone instead of normalized for consistency?
4. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the observed problems?
5. Does useful setup remain while generic throat-clearing is gone?
6. Was structure preserved unless changing it improved comprehension?
7. Are precise technical and domain terms unchanged, including established Yoke product language?

## Clarity

1. Does each generic sentence pass the portability test or carry a necessary general rule?
2. Do concrete facts, mechanisms, examples, consequences, and direct verbs do the work?
3. Are tangled sentences fixed while clear spoken cadence and fragments remain?
4. Are unsupported attributions named as unsupported rather than replaced with invented sources?

## Pattern check

1. Are empty buzzwords, filler phrases, and inflated claims removed unless quoted or used precisely?
2. Are binary contrasts, negative lists, rhetorical setups, and throat-clearing removed?
3. Are faux-insight setups, colon reveals, superficial analysis, synonym cycling, dramatic fragments,
   and robotic rhythm fixed?
4. Is importance puffery replaced with facts, and interpretive metadiscourse removed?
5. Are decorative kickers and recap endings gone?
6. Does formatting follow the content instead of decorating it?
7. Are colons grammatical and em dashes sparse?

## Final read

1. Would the writer recognize the edited draft as their own voice?
2. Would it sound natural when read to a sharp colleague?
3. Does Edit output include the full draft and **What changed**?
4. Does Detect output stop after named, quoted, actionable findings?
